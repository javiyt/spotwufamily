About

Spot Wu Family is an unofficial static catalog project for music related to Wu-Tang Clan and the wider Wu Family network.

The editorial artist list is reviewed in YAML. Spotify metadata is collected server-side, normalized into SQLite, exported as deterministic JSON and published as a static Hugo site.

This project is not officially affiliated with Wu-Tang Clan, its members, associated artists, or Spotify. Spotify metadata and outbound music links are provided by Spotify.

The concept of Wu Family has fuzzy edges. Categories, aliases and collaborators are editorial choices and should be reviewed before sync automation runs.
